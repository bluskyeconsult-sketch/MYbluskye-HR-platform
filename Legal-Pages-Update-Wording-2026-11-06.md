## Privacy Policy: add these sections

### Human check on our forms (Cloudflare Turnstile)
To stop automated sign-ups and spam, some of our forms (newsletter sign-up, contact, tester codes and, where enabled, sign-in and registration) use Cloudflare Turnstile. When you use one of these forms, Turnstile checks signals from your browser and device, such as your IP address and how your browser behaves, to decide whether you are a person. Cloudflare processes this information under its own privacy terms. We use it only to protect our services. We do not use it for advertising.

### Newsletter sign-up and confirmation
When you subscribe to our newsletter we ask for your email address and, optionally, your name. We send you an email asking you to confirm, and we only send newsletters once you confirm. You can choose which topics you receive and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. We send emails through our email provider, Brevo.

### Addresses we block or suppress
If an address unsubscribes, bounces, complains, or appears to be created by an automated process, we keep a record of that address, and the reason, on a suppression list. We do this so that we do not email it again and so that automated sign-ups cannot repeat. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in keeping our services secure and our email reputation healthy.

### Security and abuse prevention
We record the IP address of requests to some forms for a short period so that we can limit how often one address can submit them. We may block addresses or sign-ups that look automated or abusive.

### Visitor statistics
We record basic information about visits to our site, such as pages viewed, approximate location (country and city), device type, browser and how long a visit lasted, so we can understand and improve the site. We leave out visits that look like automated crawlers so our figures are accurate.

## Cookie Policy: add this

### Cookies and similar technologies
Our site uses cookies and similar browser storage for the following purposes:
- Essential: keeping you signed in and keeping the site secure.
- Security: the Cloudflare Turnstile human check may use cookies or similar storage to tell people from automated programs.
- Visitor statistics: a session identifier so we can count visits and pages.
You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site from working.

## Terms of Service: add this

### Automated access
You must not use bots, scripts or other automated means to create accounts, subscribe to our newsletter, submit forms or collect data from the platform. We may block or remove sign-ups and subscriptions that we reasonably believe are automated or abusive.

### Immigration and relocation information
Information on our Visa Pathways and Care Relocation pages and in our AI chat is general information only. It is not immigration advice. Rules, fees and thresholds change, and some content describes proposals that are not yet law. Each page shows when it was last checked. You must confirm details on the official government website, or with a regulated adviser, before you act.
